# Software logo sources

These third-party product marks are used only to identify Mohamed Hesham's design tools.

- Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Canva, and Premiere Pro: [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), MIT-licensed icon set. Product names and marks remain the property of their owners.
- InDesign: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adobe_Indesign_CC_2026_icon.svg), sourced there from Adobe and listed as a public-domain text/logo mark subject to trademark restrictions.

Do not recolor or distort the logos. Keep their original proportions and follow each brand's current usage guidelines in commercial deployments.
